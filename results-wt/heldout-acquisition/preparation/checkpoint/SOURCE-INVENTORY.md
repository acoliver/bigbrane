The selective delivery contains public exclusion metadata, stage reports, source projections, validators and tests. It does not contain raw provider caches, PDFs, corpus source bodies, source identity dossiers, full ledgers or a reference-label vault. `PUBLIC-FILES.json` lists the selected paths. `DELIVERY.json` and its SHA256 pin bind the compact inventory package without replacing either historical stage.

The current inventory supplies no global guard or independence finding.

Original stage manifests retain their original artifact hashes; not every artifact body named by those historical manifests is distributed. The delivery loader validates its own explicit subset and reconstructs alias unions without opening prior source bodies. It neither claims nor performs complete historical-stage reauthentication.

Rechecking the full original provenance requires the retained local originals. Existing inventory source tests are included as evidence and can require those unshipped files.
