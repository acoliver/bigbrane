# Independent acquisition pilot preregistration

## Scope and objective

Freeze a paired 2 x 2 experiment on 12 independently authored mechanism cards. The experimental unit is a mechanism, never a result position or a paper. Each card runs query syntax baseline Q0 and candidate Q1, each crossed with seed baseline S0 and balanced S1. All four arms receive the same concept and lens metadata and budgets. No production integration or acceptance rerun occurs here.

The motivating observations are 122 truncated historical windows, 845/848 candidates with one-query support, and 18/20 seeds appearing in the first query. Original PA2 remains FAIL at 2/4 against 3/4. Named guards remain 9/10 and 3/6. The initial 429 cause is unknown. Neither these observations nor the four previously registered queries supply held-out evidence.

## Independent corpus and blinding

A future corpus worker, separate from the policy implementer, creates 12 external mechanism descriptions across at least four subject families, at most three cards per family. No paraphrase of a registered or historical card qualifies. A separate label worker obtains independent reference works and semantic relevance judgments, including relevance rationale and identity aliases, before any arm results are visible. Store reference labels in a separate vault, pin its manifest hash, and give the acquisition worker only opaque card IDs, mechanism concept phrases and four ordered lenses with three keyword phrases each. Do not include target titles, authors, identifiers or labels in query metadata. Corpus independence needs human provenance, not a free-form boolean. Freeze corpus, metadata, vault hash and inclusion order before acquisition. Run every card once, with no replacements based on outcomes.

Exclude the 11 original PA2 registered works, all 19 historical recovery mechanisms, the 109 already seen seed mechanisms, and all other previously used study data. The future worker inventories original cards, seed/recovery manifests and prior study artifacts, adds known title, arXiv, DOI and source-ID aliases, and pins each inventory's bytes. Normalize Unicode titles, punctuation, arXiv versions/URLs, DOI URLs/case and source aliases. Check BOTH mechanism identities and reference-work identities against the registry before unblinding. Record missing aliases and unknown identity links. Passing known-identifier checks establishes only conditional disjointness, never global independence. The initial corpus is empty with PENDING reason; incomplete exclusion inventories cannot pass the corpus gate. Existing registration labels may be read after this freeze only to populate exclusions. They cannot select policy words. The four old registered queries are not held out.

## Policies and budgets

Use exactly 12 queries per source: four provided lenses in supplied order, each with three phrases. Each raw query is the concept plus its lens phrase. No adaptive query generation or refined round, target lookup, word removal, padding or query replacement. Q0 copies current generic adapter semantics: ASCII tokens joined by all-term AND on arXiv, unchanged query strings on OpenAlex and S2. Q1 retains every ASCII token and changes only arXiv to an AND of (ti:token OR abs:token) groups. OpenAlex and S2 remain unchanged because their boolean behavior is unverified. This candidate tests field scope, not token removal, provider ranking or larger windows. Reject duplicate/empty normalized queries and metadata carrying unexpected fields. Syntax deployment needs future adapter validation, not a claim made by replay.

Each arm has 36 search-operation slots, one first window per query and source, limit six, source order arxiv/openalex/s2. No local-index source is used in this remote-only pilot. Five unique seed slots follow initial acquisition. S0 ranks by distinct normalized query support then first discovery, matching the generic pool rule. S1 assigns each chosen identity to its least-used supported lens/query/source stratum: minimize lens count, then query count, then source count, then baseline rank, then supplied stratum order. Increment only that assignment, never duplicate appearances. Keep explicit alias unions and title-based merges visible as identity assumptions.

For each selected seed, schedule exactly two S2 logical calls, references then citations, each limit six, using its S2 ID, arXiv ID or DOI in that order. Thus ten expansion slots maximum. An unresolvable seed gets explicit unavailable receipts consuming those slots, not another provider. This fixed direct source is a deliberate controlled departure from production's multi-call OpenAlex lookup and fallback. No seed padding. Unfilled seed/expansion slots remain unspent. Final exposure is capped at 60 unique identities using current field-representative selection followed by distinct-query ranking and discovery ties. This selector is fixed across arms. No full-text fetch or model judgment is scheduled. Optional later fetched/used evidence must be immutable and validated; absent evidence is unknown, not zero observed usage.

Logical slots count cache hits, cooldowns and preflight-unavailable operations equally. A zero-HTTP result never creates another allowance. Count transport attempts, dispatches, responses, redirects and retries separately. Keep the existing maximum four attempts, thirty-second timeout, five-second initial backoff, 120-second cap and 600-second exhausted-429 cooldown; no hidden retry or pagination increase. A terminal network/parse/error/cooldown/unavailable result aborts that arm immediately. Remaining slots are explicitly unspent. A missing receipt is malformed input, never an empty result. Successful empty windows are valid answered operations. Do not replace failed cards. Report complete four-arm cards and incomplete cards separately, with every incomplete card retained in the fixed denominator for an intention-to-acquire sensitivity bound of zero recovery. Label unavailable cells as unavailable, not semantic failures.

## Immutable receipts and analysis

Each arm supplies its own hash-pinned input pool. Validate card, arm, call sequence/ID, query hash, exact public request parameters, source aliases, window, limit, ordered return records, terminal outcome, attempts and exchange sequence. Q0/Q1 require fresh acquisition: the same saved pool cannot estimate a query effect. S0/S1 may share immutable search bodies within Q when call ownership is explicit. Expansion inputs must match the actually selected seeds. Reject missing arms, foreign calls, extra calls, malformed counts and hash tampering. Capture pool hashes and source hashes in each result.

Primary outcome is per-card exposed relevant-reference coverage: matched distinct relevant reference identities / frozen relevant references, bounded in [0,1]. Count acquired, expansion-returned, fetched, exposed and reference-used coverage separately, with evidence for fetched/used. Reference labels are externally supplied later. Availability is answered logical calls / consumed logical calls and is not recall. Report raw counts, fixed budgets, unspent budgets, alias assumptions and pending stages alongside metrics. No absent denominator is silently converted to zero. A card with no relevant references has undefined coverage and invalidates the planned evaluable sample.

For each complete card, compute query effect separately at S0 and S1, seed effect separately at Q0 and Q1, their averages, and interaction (Q1S1-Q1S0)-(Q0S1-Q0S0). Do not attribute a joint-arm gain to both policies. Report paired card-level differences and exact sign-test counts, plus a fixed-seed 10,000-resample card bootstrap 95% interval as pilot uncertainty, not prospective power. Twelve cards is a feasibility pilot and no power claim is made.

A policy earns further independent testing only if both its fixed-other-factor mean contrasts are positive, its average-effect lower bootstrap bound exceeds zero, its mean reference-used coverage does not decrease when evidenced, and transport-complete card count does not decrease. A nonpositive mean at either fixed factor falsifies the broad improvement claim for this pilot. Other cases, absent usage evidence, missing labels, incomplete corpus or failed transport are inconclusive for that decision. Interaction is descriptive and must accompany any contrast. No pilot result triggers automatic production rollout or changes the original acceptance result. Routine future work needs no new owner-approval gate.
