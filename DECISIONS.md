Guiding rule: a verdict counts only if it rests on stated inputs and checked evidence (Lean or SymPy proof, SMT up to a bound, or executed code at the protocol sizes), and has survived an adversarial review by a different model family. ## Clarification recorded 2026-10-18: r2-bar evidence and acceptance

This dated clarification does not rewrite the original preregistered rules or historical entries. The original r2 replay named-gate outcomes remain failures: Rule 1 is 9/10 and Rule 2 is 3/6. All-dead diagnostics of 10/10 and 6/6 are not substitutes for the registered gates. PA2 retrieval remains 2/4 against 3/4.
