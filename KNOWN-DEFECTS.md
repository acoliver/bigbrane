# Known checker defects carried into the 30-idea batch (decisions SR2, SR3)

The last calibration (20261017T045735Z) ran one round under all fixes through FIX-CT. Three card-runs finished, scored on the full rule: 27 of 29 items matched. Every one of the 21 cell verdicts was right, for the transformer, the LSTM and the n-gram. K3 run 1 was still in its prover round when the calibration stopped. The two misses and one earlier defect are listed here. A batch kill whose reason involves one of them is flagged for re-examination and not counted as a clean kill.

| ID | Defect | Evidence | Kill codes to flag in the batch | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| KD1 | Learner budget measured where the learner was not validated. The card-supplied P4 learners were validated on the host. NumPy in the pinned tools container runs them several times slower, so their CPU projections exceed the 1440 s admission and the check never runs: status unknown, then `unproven:P4.<task>`. | K2 recall and composition probes project 1857 and 2386 CPU s (cpu_s 75.7 and 82.3 on 300 examples). Flag it anyway so a refusal is never read as a learning failure. | Validate calibration protocols inside the pinned runner (development seeds, then check seeds), alongside the batch. |
| KD2 | Stage C materiality replay can crash on the candidate's own fixed-size table. The replay crashes with IndexError when the replayed sizes exceed a table (256 position rows at n above 256).
