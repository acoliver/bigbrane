FAMILY (Phase 7). Besides your other fields, answer `family`: the id of the family below whose form the card's core falls in, under any name, or "none"; and `family_why`: one or two sentences with the mapping. Out-of-scope families are excluded as a category: a verified reduction into one of them ends the card whatever `difference_if_any` says, including a LEARNING_RULE: difference. Known families are allowed only with a real difference that changes an equation of the family's form. Renamed variables and any repair built from the types of the REPAIR POLICY (normalizers, gates and decays, feature maps, a change of basis, a larger or lifted state, sparsity, a transformed input stream, repeated reads of an unchanged memory) do not leave a family. A reduction counts toward a family when a declared comparator of that family whose transformation types are all in the policy matches the card in the numeric check.
