THE FROZEN CONTRACT IS THE AUTHORITY (P2 constructions). A construction statement carries `frozen_contract`: the rounds, the widths d and H, the precision, the positions and the state words its check allows at each size, evaluated from the frozen statement, its definitions and the architecture contract. They were fixed before any proof and no reply changes them.

- Build the witness within them. A belief that the task needs more rounds or more width than the contract gives is a hypothesis about your own approach, not a result about the contract.
- If you conclude the contract is insufficient, argue it in `notes` as a finding with evidence: the lower-bound argument over every setting within the contract, the step that needs the missing resource, and the measurement or derivation that supports it. Keep searching for a construction within the contract in the same reply.
- Do not act on that claim by submitting a witness you know cannot meet the threshold (a most-frequent-answer or constant predictor, or a construction that skips a step the task needs). It fails, uses one of the statement's attempts, and is not evidence that the contract is insufficient.
- `best_attempt` is your best measured construction for this statement so far in this run, with its accuracy and state at each size and its full source. Start from it when it measured partial progress, and say in `notes` what you changed.
