MECHANISM DEFINITIONS AND ANCHORED OBJECTIONS (these rules refine the Definitions rule above and decide where they differ).

You also get CARD MECHANISM: the card's own state, update, readout and learning rule, each with an anchor. Each definition carries its kind and provenance; a definition of kind "mechanism" says it restates the card's mechanism.

Definitions. A definition that faithfully restates the card's own state, update equations, readout or learning rule (the card's sizes, such as the number of states, included) defines the object the claims are about. It is part of the claim, not a hidden premise: give it verdict "mechanism", even though a different mechanism could exist. Give "convention" to a protocol or accounting choice. Give "substantive" only when the definition asserts something beyond the card: performance (an accuracy or success the claims should establish), a capacity or bound, a cost, a property of the data or of the world, or a mechanism that differs from the card's (added state, buffers or steps, a changed equation). A definition that mixes a faithful restatement with such an assertion is substantive only for that clause.

For a "substantive" verdict, fill `objection`: `definition_quote` is the exact clause you object to, copied from the definition; `kind` is "contradicts_card" when the clause changes the card's mechanism (then `card_quote` is the exact card text it changes, copied from CARD MECHANISM or the card's claims) or "beyond_card" when it asserts something the card does not state (`card_quote` null, or card text it goes beyond); `asserts` says what the clause asserts; `difference` says in one sentence what differs. An objection whose clause only restates the card's state, update, readout or learning rule is discarded by the host. For "convention" and "mechanism", `objection` is null.

Statements. Every rubric item you mark "differs", and every direction other than "match", needs an entry in `objections`: `item` (the rubric item, or "direction"), `claim_quote` (exact text of the claim: of `instantiated_claim` when the item has one, otherwise of `claim` or `claim_quote`), `statement_quote` (exact text of the formal statement or of a definition it uses) and `difference` (one sentence on what differs between the two quotes). Copy the quotes character for character; the host checks them. An objection without both quotes, or with quotes that are not exact text of their sides, is discarded and blocks nothing. When nothing differs, `objections` is an empty list.

A second reader, from another model family, adjudicates every anchored objection. Raise an objection only when you can point to the words that differ.
