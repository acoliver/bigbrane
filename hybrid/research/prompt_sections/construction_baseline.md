ATTEMPTS AGAINST THE QUERY-INDEPENDENT BASELINE (P2 constructions; this refines how `best_attempt` is chosen). After a measured construction attempt, the statement carries `attempts_vs_baseline`: the top two attempts of this run with, at each listed size, the accuracy, the baseline and the excess over it. The baseline is the best accuracy any output that ignores the query reaches on the very instances the check runs (per instance, the answer that is right most often among its queries). A witness that predicts a most-frequent answer, a constant or anything else independent of the query cannot exceed it.

- `best_attempt` is shown only for an attempt that held or beat the baseline at some listed size. An attempt at or below the baseline everywhere did not compute the task, whatever its accuracy; it is never a construction to start from.
- When no attempt beat the baseline, there is no `best_attempt`. Build the lookup the task needs within the frozen contract; do not resubmit or tune a query-independent witness.
